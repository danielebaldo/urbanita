About Urbanìta

Urbanìta is a small, city-lookup tool and a personal blog by Daniele Baldo.

What it does

Every search resolves an article on Wikipedia, then checks Wikidata to confirm the result is a settlement — a city, town, village, municipality, and so on — before showing it. If it isn’t, Urbanìta says so and offers real alternatives instead of guessing.

No build step, no backend

The whole site is static files talking directly to the Wikipedia and Wikidata APIs from your browser. No server, no database.

Built by

Vibe coded by Daniele Baldo — a city lover continuously trying to find new ways to have fun around urban things.

About Urbanìta

From the English dictionary, an urbanite is nothing but a city-dweller. Etymologically from the Latin urbanus, an urbanite is literally “a person of the city.”

This noun can actually hide multiple meanings, both on the anthropological and societal levels. Philosophically, an urbanite exists and coexists with and within the city, as there is no urbanite without the urban. In this sense, the urbanite is a state of mind and of consciousness. A person of and by the city, observing, contemplating, admiring the urban environment and its multifaceted dimensions.

Urbanìta is an Italian translation of urbanite — a deliberate coinage taken from my Stratosferica family, not to be confused with urbanità, which already exists in Italian but means something else entirely: courtesy, refinement, good manners. No dictionary offers a true equivalent of urbanite in this sense, and the usual synonyms — cittadino, abitante della città — fail to capture the deep complexity of the original word.

Urbanìta is my tribute to cities, a digital space to host my thoughts around them and those of others, while building funny things that might evolve into more serious things, or not.

Thank you for reading this.

With sympathy,

About this website

Urbanìta is a small city-lookup tool, built as static files talking directly to the Wikipedia and Wikidata APIs from your browser — no server, no database. Every search resolves an article on Wikipedia, then checks Wikidata to confirm the result is a genuine settlement — a city, town, village, municipality, and so on — before showing it. If it isn’t, Urbanìta says so and offers real alternatives instead of guessing.
